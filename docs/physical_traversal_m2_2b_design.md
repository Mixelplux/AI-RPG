## Meaningful increments and F05

One scope retains its original objective, method, constraints and explicit means.
A finite sequence of established directed links denotes meaningful physical
boundaries; each increment uses its own grounded duration and contextual ruling.
A link is not subdivided into invented handholds, percentages or six-second ticks.
Narration creates no checkpoint. Successful accepted increments advance the stage;
failed attempts retain their actual disposition and may permit a distinct new
attempt when current rules and circumstances allow it.

Continuation advances ordinary authorized work until completion, an unsuccessful
attempt, material unavailability, or an F05 boundary. Missing upstream suppliers
may submit legitimate World/Character developments between increments. They may
supply physical facts, capability/access and F05 checkpoint classifications, never
rolls or predetermined success/failure. Accepted fact/control changes use the same
root and historical ordering as movement. Original restrictions are retained and
rechecked on initial and resumed execution. Supported restriction declarations
are explicit owner-supplier contracts, not claims to interpret arbitrary language.

F05 uses legitimate perception and opportunity to respond. Unperceived developments
do not become player choices or disclose their cause. Missing material information
stays technically unresolved with safe generic output. Physical unavailability
returns control without creating a failed attempt. Previously answered risks refer
to their exact scope; a new recognizable material risk needs a fresh answer.

## Accepted identity, persistence and restoration

Do not introduce checkpoint-to-result references. Non-null resolution_ref remains
invalid, including actual accepted IDs. Accepted increment recognition retains
activity association, request meaning, historical dependency/result evidence and
safe receipt; interruption/presentation repair reuses these records without draws
or repeated costs. Changed meaning under an accepted identity rejects. A new
attempt identity alone does not waive current eligibility, completion or F05.

Keep save envelopes 1/2/3 and historical domain schemas 1/2 unchanged. New opt-in
domain schema 3 represents continuity records and ordered owner-fact/control
updates. Every recognizable player decision or objectionable discovery-risk
interruption needs a unique response-addressable key and a nonempty jointly
accessible notice. Duplicate keys, missing notices, and notices without legitimate
perception/opportunity reject. Responses bind to that activity's current notices
and exact revision. Hidden developments acquire neither notices nor answers.

Every keyed non-ordinary development, including a hidden one, carries one internal
`ChoiceContext(key, source, stages)`. `source` names this activity's original scope
or the accepted owner update that first establishes that particular occurrence;
`stages` names zero-based occurrences in the already established activity plan.
These are references to existing meaningful stages, not finer gameplay increments.
A new occurrence must name its actual establishing record. Reusing a key cannot
change its occurrence, classification, objectionability or declared stage scope.
The original declaration may cover several stages, including separate visits to
the same route; it cannot be broadened after the player answers.

Pure validation reconstructs the selected stages' authoritative climbing failure
circumstances and rule stakes (check requirement, failure mode and expenditure).
A relevant change while a scoped decision remains current or ahead requires a
fresh key/context and, when recognizable and answerable, a fresh response. This
also applies to fact-only changes, changes contributed for another activity, and
the historical reconstruction path. It does not depend on notice-text equality,
World revision, elapsed time, unrelated owner changes or already completed stages.
The first notice is still retained conservatively; paraphrase equivalence is not
inferred. Stable choices keep their answers across checkpoint replacement,
temporary absence, explicit multi-stage scope, resumption and save/load. Expired
context-bound answers cannot acquire new mechanical risk authority. Bare original
answers must identify either an established context or an original mechanical risk
on the authorized path; the latter retains its published mechanical scope only.

Contexts stay internal, including occurrence references and hidden risk facts.
Visible choices still require complete accessible notices; hidden choices acquire
neither notices nor responses. Suppliers remain responsible for legitimate
observation/access, identifying distinct non-mechanical occurrences and complete
applicable facts. This is not a semantic interpreter or a general observer model.
Ordinary changes to surfaces/capacity still use current owning rules and eligibility;
a newly meaningful decision must be declared by those owners.

The accepted domain-schema-3 records require complete `choice_contexts` fields
on ActivityScope/ActivityUpdate and finite typed ChoiceContext records. Missing or
historically inconsistent context rejects, including superseded unaccepted schema-3
drafts; no text-based association is synthesized. Save envelopes 1/2/3 and published
domain schemas 1/2 keep their meanings, with the single named empty ActivityScope
context default for old bytes. No migration, additional store or save version is
introduced.

Changed risk identifiers retain their historical meaning across all updates.
The finite decoder supplies only explicitly named default additions for
old schemas; new schema records require all fields. Reject continuity vocabulary
under old schemas instead of silently promoting their historical meaning.

Validate complete reconstruction from historical owner facts and recorded dice,
including every fact/control update, authorization, stage association and safe
receipt, before one live-root adoption. No sampling or rewriting history during
restoration. Proposals bind to the captured root; stale/malformed updates publish
no fictional effect. Existing atomic file writes remain unchanged. No exhaustive
event ledger, storage technology, migration, cross-process exactly-once or
cryptographic save authenticity is introduced.

## Verification and boundaries

Cover all 16 owner-requested structural/continuing-play categories with varied
valid World/Character premises; retain accepted regressions and affected-system
checks. Live providers are forbidden. Standalone production CLI input, other
methods, general environment/NPC simulation, new protection configurations,
unsupported reactions and later death-save gameplay remain outside scope.
Growing-history reconstruction and whole-snapshot storage remain known limits.
