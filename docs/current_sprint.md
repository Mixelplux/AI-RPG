## One coherent milestone

Deliver a bounded
single-player investigation with one grounded custodian and one persistent
dispatch ledger: inquiry, an authorized social approach, legitimate evidence
examination, accepted consequences and durable recovery form one coherent loop.

The player owns consequential methods and commitments under F05. Routine
dialogue may serve an authorized activity, but paying, promising, threatening,
protected disclosure or materially changing approach requires the player's
authorization. A request for information or feasibility does not itself
commit to a consequential attempt.

Custodian behavior follows legitimate knowledge, beliefs, motivations, duties
and circumstances. Apply actual D&D 2024 Influence rules where applicable,
covering willing, hesitant and unwilling outcomes; use Search/Study only where
applicable to legitimately accessible evidence. Do not derive these mechanics
from legacy climbing/gate fixtures or treat a test contract fixture as gameplay.

Owning gameplay domains establish and coherently accept external facts,
actor responses, costs, time, discoveries and character knowledge. Separate
objective truth, beliefs, NPC claims, reports, inference, hidden and unspecified
information throughout resolution and presentation. Preserve accepted
foundational Design and all existing authority boundaries.

## M1 gameplay completion condition

M1 is complete only when the bounded investigation demonstrates all of the
following and its verification and documentation reconciliation are complete:

1. A safe interface for interpreted player intentions preserves inquiry versus
   an authorized undertaking, target, approach, prerequisites and player control.
   Controlled offline interpretations must reach owning-domain adjudication and
   authoritative acceptance, with clarification where needed. Actual LLM language
   understanding is not an M1 acceptance requirement. Genuine contextual language
   interpretation remains an accepted whole-game requirement for early API
   integration, before substantial expansion into additional gameplay domains.
2. One grounded custodian supports applicable willing, hesitant and unwilling
   Influence cases, with outcomes justified by rules and circumstances rather
   than narration inventing knowledge, permission or cooperation.
3. Accessible ledger evidence can be examined using applicable Search/Study;
   lack of access cannot be bypassed by narration or a check. Findings retain
   their legitimate source and epistemic status.
4. Consequential responses and findings are accepted coherently by their owning
   domains. Failed/rejected acceptance cannot publish partial changes or confer
   character knowledge. Presentation preserves character access, attribution
   and uncertainty without exposing hidden or inventing unspecified facts.
5. Accepted results, costs, time and relevant NPC/evidence continuity survive
   save/load and recovery. Recognized retries do not reroll, duplicate effects,
   erase consequences or reinterpret established results.
6. Focused offline tests and affected-system regressions demonstrate the above;
   targeted ownership review and compatibility/recovery checks preserve V3,
   common schema 4, historical schemas 1–3 and unchanged V1/V2 behavior,
   including corruption rejection and atomic adoption. Owner smoke covers the
   player-visible investigation before acceptance; documentation reflects the
   submitted behavior without claiming premature acceptance.
