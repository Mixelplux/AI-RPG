# Research

Research investigates feasibility, limitations and alternative approaches.
Experimental results provide evidence, not automatic design acceptance,
architectural acceptance or production requirements. Acceptance of experimental
findings does not imply acceptance of the tested implementation.

Existing records remain at their original paths:

- [Research precedent index](../architecture_comprehension_review.md) — existing studies, evidence locations and their limits.
- [Deferred findings register](../deferred_findings.md) — unresolved limitations and research questions with their original sources.
