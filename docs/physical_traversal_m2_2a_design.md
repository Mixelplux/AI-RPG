## Consequence classification before randomness

World supplies explicit route-local support during the check: load-bearing footing
or grip-dependent exposure; actual landing, drop distance and medium; protection
(or verified absence), assistance (only explicit none supported), causal basis and
unresolved bindings. These are physical premises, not an on-failure scenario answer.
Resolution requires a trusted contextual check-stakes ruling: failed progress on
load-bearing support, or failure to maintain grip during a grip-dependent attempt.
A mismatch or omitted material classification rejects before drawing. This is a
bounded CD-02 contextual ruling, not a universal inference from a failed Athletics
roll. Choosing a progress-only check cannot silently sanitize an exposed climb.

World independently derives the physical disposition: supported no progress;
lost grip held by verified taut protection; or actual fall to the previously
established landing. Ground-level footing cannot create a fall from height.
A short established drop can be an actual fall without damage under the core rule.
No second fall-chance roll. No new support/anchor/landing is created after sampling.
Protection is narrowly supported only as an already taut, load-bearing system:
intact rope/harness/anchor, anchor secured to a place, rope secured to anchor and
attached to harness, harness attached to creature, known supported position, and
Character readiness. Slack catches, uncertain strength, assistance, mitigation,
water entry and other unsupported material causes block before any check.

F05 remains operative. Exposed uncertain risk requires the exact pre-established
risk reference to be answered in the scoped authorization; checkpoint choices,
means, restrictions and intent still gate execution. Read-only assessment never
samples. Only execution samples once, after all possible branches are supported.
The trusted contextual ruling must explicitly establish the failed-attempt movement
commitment (from zero through the available planned cost) before drawing; the
scoped increment duration is also fixed. Success spends the actual climbing cost.
Failure spends the declared effort, rather than assuming the entire attempted
distance was traversed or imposing a universal failure tax or six-second turn. No partial-link model.

## Injury and zero HP

Character owns current HP and an optional explicit vitality profile: maximum HP,
temporary HP, zero-HP treatment (character/death saves or monster/instant death),
stability, death-save counters and dead state. Sparse old deterministic profiles
remain valid. Damaging fall branches require these premises before sampling.
Character computes damage absorption, remaining HP, Prone, Unconscious and immediate
death where applicable. Unconsciousness implies Incapacitated/Prone and inability
to move; World drops held objects at the accepted final position without erasing
independent attachments. Damage while already at zero can record the immediate
failure/unstable/death consequence; no later death-save turn, healing, stabilization,
combat or recovery executor is added. Unsupported active effects fail closed.
A zero-HP/dead/prone creature cannot silently continue ordinary climbing.

Official 2024 references checked October 26, 2026:
[Playing the Game](https://www.dndbeyond.com/sources/dnd/br-2024/playing-the-game#DamageandHealing)
and [Rules Glossary](https://www.dndbeyond.com/sources/dnd/br-2024/rules-glossary#Falling).
Falls use capped d6 Bludgeoning and damage-dependent Prone. Temporary HP absorb
first. Zero HP invokes the declared treatment; excess reaching maximum HP kills.
Unconsciousness entails dropped held items. Subsequent death-save gameplay is deferred.

## Acceptance, random evidence and restoration

One immutable Campaign root and the existing Runtime publication protocol remain.
World, Character and Resolution validate their contributions before the common
root comparison/assignment. All branch sufficiency checks precede draws; a stale,
malformed or technically rejected proposal has no fictional consequences. Draws
remain provisional until coherent acceptance, consistent with existing accepted
technical-rejection semantics; no pending-roll store or M2-2b behavior is added.
Exact accepted retries return stored receipts before owner reads or randomness.

Extract pure mechanical evaluators that accept recorded rolls. Validation discovers
historical dependencies from each historical domain state, checks roll count,
exact type and bounds, recomputes totals, success, fall damage and owner effects,
then compares the complete unit and reconstructed aggregate. It never invokes a
random callback, even with a replay sequence. Plausible valid dice cannot prove
external authenticity: the trusted runtime controls draws; save validation proves
internal coherence, not cryptographic tamper evidence.

Keep save envelope v3; add opt-in domain schema 2 for the new semantics. Schema 1
remains deterministic with its prior meaning. Decode only a finite explicit set
of omitted legacy default fields for schema-1 files, so existing valid v3 bytes
load without migration. Schema 2 requires all encoded fields. Reject unknown
schema, record, field, references, unsupported effects and incoherent results
before live adoption. Existing v1/v2 behavior and atomic writes remain unchanged.
Initial premises are historical validation evidence, not a competing World root.

## Owner-authorized independent-review correction addendum — October 26, 2026

The original independent review found three P2 defects. The owner separately
allowed their correction within M2-2a and the existing 24-file inventory; accepted
Design, lifecycle, save versions and publication authority are unchanged.

World must prove prospective causal-record and actual-fall identities are available
across its full identity namespace before any draw. The local fixed-anchor and worn-
harness roles in the supported taut configuration reject contradictory authoritative
dispositions at construction/restoration and before execution. An explicitly located
worn harness follows its wearer on accepted movement; the protected arrival branch
is validated before sampling. These checks do not constrain an extended rope's
legitimate connections to several locations or implement handling/release actions.

Character distinguishes damage that reduces positive HP to zero (massive-damage
overflow after temporary HP) from damage taken while already at zero (actual damage
for the death threshold). Temporary HP still buffer HP loss; nonzero damage at zero
still records the immediate death-save failure, instability and any immediate death.

The owner's simplicity guardrail is preserved: ordinary mechanical climbing needs
no protection records, individual handholds, pitons or equipment micro-simulation.
The taut rope/harness/anchor arrangement is one supported protection configuration.
Other legitimate configurations remain future bounded vocabulary/rule extensions
in the owning domains, codec and safe projection; they need no change to coherent
acceptance or the single-root publication contract. No other protection method,
general physics or new generic abstraction is introduced by these corrections.
