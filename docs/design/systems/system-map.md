## Integrated gameplay architecture — accepted design

[Integrated Gameplay Architecture V1](../integrated-gameplay-architecture-v1.md)
consolidates owner-accepted IG-01–IG-18 and IG-20–IG-23; IG-19 is not accepted.

extends a common reusable runtime only when separately authorized and staged.

Coordination interprets and manages executable undertaking scope, ordinary
constituent steps and continuation boundaries. Authoritative scene circumstances,
including geography, actors, environment and time, ground situated progression;
responsible domains establish any legitimate additional specificity. Player-owned
direction, method and risk choices retain F05 control boundaries. Coordination
acquires no independent authority over world facts, mechanical outcomes or NPC
decisions and is not an additional outcome-owning system.

Domains normally identify their applicable information requirements, including
relationships, innate capabilities, equipment and active effects; Information
Foundation supplies direct and relationship-based retrieval. The coordinator
facilitates that flow. Accepted consequential facts enter their owning domains
and remain retrievable. Domain validation and coherent acceptance preserve
dependent consequences, current-state validity, retry safety and durable
recognition, including recovery/save-load continuity in the target design.
F01 still receives only legitimate accessible meaning. Conceptual responsibilities
mandate neither separate runtime modules nor individual AI calls; see the
[contract reconciliation](../integrated-gameplay-architecture-v1.md#contract-reconciliation-and-responsibility-relationships).

## Responsibility hierarchy

Information Resolution Core V1a is an accepted read-only cross-system information
module, not an additional outcome-deciding system. Authored Content supplies
foundations; gameplay owners supply current/history and binding meaning; Character
System supplies legitimate access. Trusted internal retrieval includes hidden
constraints, while character retrieval filters records and traversal edges by
supplied grants. Production integrations and source authentication are absent;
existing owners and F01/F05 remain unchanged. See the [V1a handoff](../../information_resolution_core_v1a_handoff.md).

| Group | Systems and responsibilities |
|---|---|
| Core Gameplay | **World Simulation:** external reality and legitimate change. **Character System:** character capability, perspective, and memory. **Action & Resolution:** adjudication of attempted action. **Actors & Social Dynamics:** meaningful intentional NPC/faction decisions. |
| Experience | **Narrative Experience:** selective, context-aware expression of authoritative truth and outcomes, preserving epistemic meaning and continuity. **Player Presentation:** player input/display and interaction surface. |
| Content | **Scenario & Authored Content:** authored foundations, constraints, and bounded declarations. |
| Cross-Cutting | **Persistence:** durable storage, restoration, and compatibility. |
| Supporting Infrastructure | **Narrator Provider / Model Adapter:** untrusted model transport and adaptation. **Verification / Evaluation:** evidence and checks. **Runtime / Tooling:** execution and development support. |

Under [Authority Composition](README.md#authority-composition), storage in
`world_state`, orchestration in `GameEngine`, shared candidate transitions and
publication, persistence, and projection do not transfer conceptual authority.
Each system retains the meaning it establishes. The hierarchy does not require
splitting containers or orchestration into new modules.

[Causal Continuity](README.md#causal-continuity) connects authoritative state,
choices, adjudicated outcomes, elapsed time, and supported causal processes.
Downstream progression stays with the appropriate owner, even within one shared
transition. Persistence/history preserve enough provenance when future reasoning
requires established causal meaning; this does not prescribe exhaustive event
sourcing, continuous simulation, a generalized causal engine, or a scheduler.

## F01–F05 design integration boundaries

**Accepted design; implementation not authorized here.**
[F05 — Authorized Activities and Player Control](../f05-authorized-activities-and-player-control.md)
is the canonical authority for D1–D6 and the accepted clarifications. It defines
the player's authorization and return of control across existing responsibilities,
not a ninth system, scheduler or new architectural layer.
[F01](../f01-truthful-presentation-and-epistemic-fidelity.md) retains its
information and fidelity requirements. This is the bounded F01–F05 integration
boundary supplied by F05; the reference baseline contains no separate canonical
F02–F04 design records, and no decisions for them are inferred here.

| Responsibility | Retained boundary under F05 |
|---|---|
| Human player / Player Presentation | Player owns consequential choices; input distinguishes inquiry, preparation and commitment in context. Return control at meaningful player-owned decisions with a legitimate opportunity to respond, including discovery-sensitive interruption. No special command syntax is required by the design. |
| Action & Resolution | Establish eligible attempts, resolution, costs and results using available prerequisites. Mechanical resolution, fictional execution and durable changes differ; an already resolved attempt is not rerolled merely because its presentation paused. |
| World Simulation | Establish external developments, elapsed time, physical changes and traces. Avoiding personal detection does not establish that evidence of the activity went unnoticed. |
| Character System | Supply established capability, legitimate means and informational access. Competent execution creates no missing prerequisites; unperceived developments need not interrupt or disclose hidden truth. |
| Actors & Social Dynamics | Establish meaningful NPC decisions, observations, reports and responses. Routine dialogue may accompany an activity; independent consequential player commitments or disclosures return control. |
| Narrative Experience / F01 | Express supplied authoritative, character-accessible information. Realization cannot execute another action, invent consequential progress or agreements, repair failure, or grant knowledge of hidden developments. |
| Scenario & Authored Content | Supply bounded circumstances and supported interaction material. Predetermined implementation/evaluation fixtures must satisfy F01's information contract; they do not relax it or require complete upstream systems. |
| Persistence | Preserve supported established meaning across restoration; F05 selects no new storage, progress checkpoint, save version or migration. Gameplay owners determine what resumption means under current circumstances. |

## Dependencies and information flow around Character System

The [Character System manifest](character-system.md) refines capability,
perspective, and memory into capability/constraints, informational access, and
selective retention/continuity. This working decomposition remains provisional.
World Simulation supplies what exists and its externally observable properties;
Character System establishes character-specific capability, informational access
or basis, and relevant retained continuity. Legitimate accessible views may be
derived jointly from both. Narrative Experience selects and frames information
within that view; Player Presentation displays it. Character System does not
independently establish all perception, and world truth alone does not grant
character knowledge. This preserves the sparse-epistemic/familiarity direction,
not a claim of general runtime access filtering.
