# Design

Design maintains intended gameplay and owner-accepted decisions. Accepted design
is distinct from provisional research and descriptions of implemented behavior.
Existing production mechanics are not presumed to be the intended design.
Each source retains its stated status and scope; provisional mechanisms and
hypotheses do not become accepted decisions through this index.

Existing sources remain at their original paths:

- [Story-First Design Doctrine](../story_first_design_doctrine.md) — accepted doctrine, provisional reasoning and hypotheses distinguished within the document.
- [Simulation principles](../simulation_principles.md) and [Simulation Model](../simulation_model.md) — design direction and conceptual world behavior.
- [Architecture decisions (ADRs)](../decisions.md) — recorded decisions and their status.
- [System manifests and shared doctrines](systems/README.md) and [system map](systems/system-map.md) — responsibilities, relationships and distinctions between accepted design, implementation and deferred questions.
